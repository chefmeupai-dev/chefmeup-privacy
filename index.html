Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Chef Me Up AI ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the App, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your email address and name when you create an account or sign in.
  • Profile Information: Your cuisine preferences, dietary restrictions, cooking habits, and kitchen equipment. This enables personalized recipe recommendations.
  • Food Photos: When you use camera-based features, photos of your ingredients or dishes are sent to our service providers for analysis. These photos are not stored after processing.
  • Usage Data: Your cooking history, saved meals, recently viewed meals, and search terms.
  • Device Information: Basic device data such as operating system version, collected automatically by the platforms we use.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality, including recipe generation and meal recommendations.
  • Personalize your experience based on your profile and preferences.
  • Manage subscriptions and process payments through our service providers.
  • Track your cooking activity and progress.
  • Improve the App's functionality and user experience.
  • Monitor usage and diagnose technical issues.
  • Communicate with you about updates or changes to the App.

3. Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our App, provide services on our behalf, or assist in analyzing how our App is used. These third parties have access to your data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

Types of service providers we use include cloud database and authentication providers, AI and image processing services, subscription management platforms, and error tracking tools. Each service provider has its own privacy policy governing their use of your data.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers and cached locally on your device to enable offline functionality. We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App. You can clear local data by deleting the App or using the "Delete Account" option within the App.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We share your data only with our service providers as described above, and solely for the purposes described in this policy. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access and Correction: You can view and edit your profile at any time in the App's settings.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen using the "Delete Account" option. This permanently removes your data from our systems.
  • Opt-Out: You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the App from your device.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary to provide you with the App and for the purposes set out in this policy. When you delete your account, your data is permanently removed. We may retain usage data for internal analysis to improve the App's functionality and security.

8. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the App by navigating to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your personal data, including your profile, saved meals, cooking history, uploaded photos, and meal preferences.

If you are unable to access the App, you may also request deletion by emailing us at support@chefmeupai.com. Please include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 business days and confirm once your data has been deleted.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Your data is transmitted over secure connections and stored in encrypted databases. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date below and notify you by posting the new policy within the App. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
